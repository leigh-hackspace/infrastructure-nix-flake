## What was broken, and what was done

1. **`*.int.leighhack.org` was NXDOMAIN.** gw is authoritative for the zone and serves
   vhost names from `/var/lib/dnsmasq/names.hosts`, which a fresh box creates **empty**.
   dns-sync could not fill it (it is OPNsense-shaped and its pinned host key is the old
   box's). Restored by hand from the old box's archive
   (`~/tmp/opnsense-archive-20261017/dnsmasq-hosts` on services1, 183 lines) and
   `kill -HUP` to dnsmasq. **This is imperative state, not in git, and nothing
   refreshes it.**
2. **No inbound NAT at all.** Every `81.187.195.17`/`.18` service timed out from the
   internet and from the LAN (no reflection). Added `machines/gw/firewall.nix`'s
   `dstnat` chain (the old box's `rdr` table), `ct status dnat accept` in forward, a
   hairpin `masquerade`, and `DMZ_NET` in `markLocalV4` (the mark runs at `mangle`,
   before `dstnat`; without it hairpinned packets went out the uplink).
3. **dnsmasq stopped sending unsolicited RAs** after `router-lan` lost carrier at
   17:14:30 (the cutover cabling). Clients lose their IPv6 default when the 1200 s
   router lifetime expires — 20 minutes later — and dual-stack names hang on IPv6
   before falling back. `systemctl restart dnsmasq` restored the timer; verified with
   `journalctl -u dnsmasq | grep RTR-ADVERT(vlan225)` and services1's
   `ip -6 route show default` refreshing.

Verified from services1 (a LAN client, so the public addresses exercise the hairpin):

```

id.leighhack.org              IPv4 302   IPv6 302
```

`just check` now passes **on gw** with zero `rustc`; `just fmt`/`fmt-check` no longer use

### 3. Make the RA/link-flap failure impossible to miss

A `router-lan` link flap stops dnsmasq's unsolicited RA timer until dnsmasq restarts;
WiFi clients then lose IPv6 20 minutes later. Either:

- add a hook that restarts dnsmasq when `router-lan` regains carrier (networkd
  dispatcher / a small systemd unit), **or**
- move router advertisements to `radvd`, which re-advertises on link-up.

At minimum add a check (gatus or a timer) that the RTR-ADVERT cadence and a host's IPv6
default are fresh, and put "restart dnsmasq after cabling" in the cutover runbook.
`machines/gw/README.md`, "The third way dnsmasq breaks the LAN silently".

### 4. Port network-status to gw

## Invariants that caused trouble today

- **Never build on gw.** `nix develop` compiles the pinned `wasm-bindgen-cli` with
  cargo, and gw has no remote builder. `just check`/`just fmt` are safe; the build-shaped
  recipes refuse via `_build-host-guard` (`INFRA_ALLOW_BUILD_ON_ROUTER=1` overrides).
- **Restart dnsmasq after cabling on the router.** It is not automatic, and the damage
  appears 20 minutes later.
- **`/var/lib/dnsmasq/names.hosts` is mutable and load-bearing.** tmpfiles only
  creates it when missing; nothing in the flake rewrites it. dns-sync now maintains it
  — read-before-write, prove-the-diff, `mv -f`, reload, re-read — but a *deleted* file
  is not rebuilt by anything, and dns-sync refuses to run rather than inventing one.
  Snapshot it by hand before editing it outwith the tool.
- **Confirm every switch/boot in the same shell invocation** (`just switch` does this).
  All machines still run `system.autoRollback`.
- **Neither `gw` nor services1 has `dig`** (nor `openssl`, nor `python3`). To check what
  the router answers, use `getent ahosts <name>` — it goes through the same resolver, so
  it proves dnsmasq reloaded and answers, which reading the file does not.
- **Someone else is working on these boxes.** services1's checkout moved from
  `055b144` to `60e7f50` under me at 18:26 (a `pull`, from an interactive session), and
  `config.xml` appeared on `gw` at 18:25. Re-read `git log -1` and `who` on a box
  before deploying to it, and do not be surprised that the handover's "already
  deployed" notes are a race.
